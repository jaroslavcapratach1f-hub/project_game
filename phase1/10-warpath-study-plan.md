# 10 — Warpath Study Plan (decision D26)

**Goal:** understand how Warpath's systems actually feel in play, so our
design keeps what made the client stay for over a year and avoids what made
him quit (ref 05). Its findings tune the numbers in 02, 03 and 06; the
structure of the blueprint doesn't depend on it.

## 1. IP hygiene (read first)

- Write notes **in our own words**. Never copy text, item names, art, icons
  or screen layouts into the project.
- Screenshots stay in `docs/visual-reference/` (already git-ignored, never
  distributed).
- Their numbers are **reference only**. Our values come from our own balance
  model (02 §6).
- We study **mechanics and pacing**, then design our own presentation (C1).

## 2. Sessions

| # | Focus | Questions to answer | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New account, first session | How fast are the first upgrades? What does the tutorial teach, in what order? When does the first pay offer appear? | 02 §5, 07 tutorial |
| 2 | The first days | Where does progress slow down first: materials, resources or research? How long are typical research timers? | 02 §3–4 |
| 3 | Gathering and the map | Truck count and how it grows; farm levels; competition at farms; march times | 02 §7, 06 §6.1 |
| 4 | Combat on the map | Formations, flanking, target switching, what a phone player can realistically control | 05 |
| 5 | Alliance and territory | Joining, territory building, forts / strongholds, rallies, what keeps members active | 06 §6.2–6.3 |
| 6 | Relocation and defeat | Convoy feel and speed, what happens after a base is defeated, shields | 06 §6.4 |
| 7 | Shop and gacha | Card pack odds display, pity, VIP perks, Monthly Card, where spending pressure feels unfair | 03 |
| 8 | Late game (videos, guides, community) | A new account won't reach late game; study it from public material: server merges, seasons, why players quit | 06 §6.7, 03 §5 |

## 3. Note template (one per session)

```
Session #, date
What happened (own words)
Pacing numbers observed (reference only)
Felt good / felt bad (and why)
What Blitzmire should do differently
Questions for the client
```

## 4. Questions for the client (his year of experience is the best source)

1. Which three moments in Warpath made you want to keep playing?
2. Which three moments made you feel forced to pay?
3. What did your alliance do on a normal day?
4. What happened to your server over time (merges, players leaving)?
5. Which features did you never use?
