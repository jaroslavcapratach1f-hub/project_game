## Your decisions (locked in)
- Building upgrades: **instant, no timers** (the Warpath way). Progress is
  limited by resources and construction materials.
- **Timers** on research and material production.
- Main spending areas: **troop cards** (star levels) → **officer cards**
  (skills) → **officer equipment** (battle stats).
- Quality and fun come first. We take the time needed to get it right.

## Phase 1: Planning & Architecture
- Final technical architecture: game, server and database
- Economy design: resource and material flow, research and production
  timers, balance targets
- **Monetisation design:** troop cards and star levels, officer cards and
  skills, officer equipment, drop rates, a fair path for free players,
  app-store compliance
- Art direction plus a 3D asset guide for buildings, tanks and officers
- Battlefield design: formations, flanking, tactical AI
- Version 1 scope and full roadmap
- Project setup: version control and code checks
- *(added later)* World map, alliance and territory design
- *(added later)* Operating cost and business setup overview

*Result: a complete blueprint. No game code in this phase.*

## Phase 2: Quality Sample
- New 3D graphics foundation, optimised for phones
- One hero building with 3 visual upgrade stages and animations
- One fully animated tank and one animated infantry squad
- Flanking battle prototype
- Tested on real phones

*Result: proof the game can look great and run smoothly.*

## Phase 3: Base & Economy
- Launch buildings with visible upgrades (instant)
- Resource gathering, construction materials, offline earnings
- Research and material production with timers
- Building animations (machinery, smoke, activity)

## Phase 4: Army, Officers & Battlefield
- Troop cards and star-level system
- Officer cards and skill unlocks
- Officer equipment and stats
- Formations, group orders, flanking, tactical AI
- Fully detailed, animated units

## Phase 5: Server, Accounts & Missions
- Accounts, cloud saving and anti-cheat
- Secure shop and card purchases
- Tutorial, missions and the first story chapter

## Phase 6: Polish & Launch
- Testing, speed tuning, iOS and Android apps
- Analytics and crash reporting
- Store approval, then a soft launch in a few countries

## Phase 7: After Launch (ongoing)
- Shared multiplayer world, PvP, alliances, events, new troops and officers,
  guided by player data
- *(now partly moved into v1 — see file 03)*

**How we work:** each phase ends with a review of the result before the next
one starts.
