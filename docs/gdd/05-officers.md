OFFICER / COMMANDER SYSTEM (DETAILED)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OVERVIEW:
Officers are named historical commanders assigned to troops.
Each officer provides passive stat buffs and active combat skills
that trigger automatically during battle. Officers are the
primary progression and customization layer for troops.

ACQUISITION:
- Officer Crates (separate from troop crates in Armory)
- Uses Arms Coupons or dedicated Officer Vouchers
- Same pity system as troops (guaranteed high-star at threshold)
- Duplicate officers convert to Officer Shards for promotion
- Some officers available through event rewards or Black Market

STAR TIERS:
- 3★ Officer: Common. 1 active skill, 1 passive skill. Max L30.
- 4★ Officer: Rare. 2 active skills, 2 passive skills. Max L50.
- 5★ Officer: Legendary. 2 active skills, 3 passive skills. Max L70.
- Golden variants: Enhanced skill effects (future expansion).

ASSIGNMENT:
- Each troop can have 1 Primary + 1 Secondary officer
- Primary officer: 100% skill effectiveness
- Secondary officer: 70% skill effectiveness
- Officers must match troop faction for full bonus:
  * Same faction: 100% effectiveness
  * Different faction: 50% effectiveness
- Officers can be reassigned freely (no cooldown)
- Unassigned officers sit in Officer Barracks

LEVELING:
- Officers gain XP from every battle their assigned troop fights
- XP amount scales with battle difficulty and duration
- Each level grants +0.5% to all stats of assigned troop
- Skill unlocks at specific level milestones:
  * 3★: Active at L1, Passive at L10
  * 4★: Active 1 at L1, Passive 1 at L5, Active 2 at L20, Passive 2 at L35
  * 5★: Active 1 at L1, Passive 1 at L5, Active 2 at L15,
         Passive 2 at L30, Passive 3 at L50

PROMOTION (Star Upgrade):
- Requires Officer Shards (from duplicate pulls or events)
- 3★ → 4★: 50 shards
- 4★ → 5★: 150 shards
- Promotion resets level to 1 but increases stat scaling per level

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OFFICER SYNERGY & DUO SKILLS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FACTION MATCHING:
- Same faction officer + troop = 100% skill effectiveness
- Different faction = 50% effectiveness
- Two same-faction officers on one troop = +10% all stats bonus

DUO SKILLS (Future Expansion):
- Specific officer pairs unlock unique combined abilities
- Example: Rommel + Guderian = "Blitzkrieg Doctrine"
  (+25% speed and damage to all tanks in range)
- Example: Patton + Bradley = "American Way of War"
  (+20% infantry and tank coordination bonus)
- Example: Zhukov + Rokossovsky = "Soviet Steamroller"
  (+30% area damage to all squads)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OFFICER BARRACKS (BUILDING)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Separate building in base
- Shows all owned officers with portraits, levels, stars, skills
- Drag-and-drop assignment to troops
- Officer detail screen: full stat breakdown, skill descriptions
- "Auto-Assign" button: best officer match for selected troop
- Training room: assign officers to training for passive XP gain
- Level increases max officer capacity and training speed
- **Recruit** (the officer gacha) is opened from here and from the Officers
  nav panel (GDD 23).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BUILDING ASSIGNMENT (logistics officers)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Some officers have a **logistics** tag and can be seated in a production or
  support building instead of a squad, granting a passive there:
  * Resource buildings — +% output of that resource.
  * Barracks / Factory — +% production speed.
  * Research Center — +% research speed.
  * Hospital — +% heal rate / capacity.
  * Recon Post — +recon range / duration.
- One officer per building; reassigning is free (no cooldown). A logistics
  officer seated in a building is not available to a squad and vice-versa.
- Same-camp seating gives the full bonus; off-camp 50% (as with squads).
